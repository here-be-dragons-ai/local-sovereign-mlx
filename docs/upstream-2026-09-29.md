## What 0.7.4 contains, for this setup

`git diff v0.7.3 v0.7.4`: `apc.py`, `apc_adapters.py`, `apc_coordinator.py`,
`models/base.py` and `speculative/` are **unchanged**. On the path:

- `#2357` in `models/qwen3_5/language.py`: the cached left-padding info is gone;
  on a single-token decode with an array `left_padding` the pads are now read
  with `.tolist()` every step. Cost on this profile: none measurable (below).
- `#2358` in `sample_utils.py` / `server/generation.py`: `clamp_temperature`
  and a rewritten top-p in `_PositionedTargetSampler`, the verify sampler.
- `#2342` in `models/cache.py`: packed width of empty 3/5/6-bit quantized KV.
  We use 8 bit.
- `#2344` / `#2365` / `#2369`: tool-parser and streaming fixes. The one that
  matters here: a `qwen3_coder` call whose tool schema has a boolean property
  (`"x": true`, valid JSON Schema) raised inside the parser, and
  `process_tool_calls` dropped it — the client received neither a tool call nor
  the text. Verified fixed with exactly such a schema.
- `#2362` reworks `prompt_utils.py` (interleaved images); `0011` still applies.

No dependency changes; `uv pip check` clean. All eight patches apply to
`v0.7.4` and to `main @ 967bf90` without rejects.

## Greedy output, and a trap in how it was checked

The `/tmp/hashcheck.py` instrument (README excerpt at `7a0e703`, `X ` prefix,
250 tokens, `temperature 0`, no drafter) gives `31b90e180e38` on 0.7.2 and
0.7.3. **Run it against a fresh `STATE_DIR`.** Against the live one it
restores the 09-25 snapshot from the SSD tier, so the prefill of the version
under test never runs, and a KV computed by one mlx version gets decoded by
another. That produced a third hash (`21b64b33ab1e`) that belongs to neither
version. Hashes from different prefixes are not comparable either: `Q `, `R `,
`S `, `T ` all give different texts, and two of them happened to coincide.

Clean, cold then warm, fresh `STATE_DIR` each:

| config | cold | warm |
|---|---|---|
| 0.7.4, mlx 0.32.2 | `31b90e180e38` | `31b90e180e38` |
| 0.7.4, mlx 0.32.3 | `4d8ff2347f46` | `4d8ff2347f46` |

**0.7.4 is bit-identical to 0.7.3.** mlx 0.32.3 is not bit-identical to
0.32.2: the text diverges at character 553 on a near-tie (`möglich wird` vs
`möglich ist`), the content after that is the same. Numerics, not quality;
`#4511` (floating-point constant precision in compiled kernels) is the
likeliest source, not traced.

## mlx 0.32.3: why the pin stays at 0.32.2

- `#4505` (D256 SDPA memory, Qwen3.8-27B peak 46.9 → 35.5 GB in the PR) changes
  the **steel** kernel, i.e. "M4 and older". On the M5 Pro D256 goes to
  `sdpa_full_self_attention_nax`; nothing to gain.
- `#4416` is in 0.32.3: default dispatch now runs D256 with an array mask
  through the fused NAX kernel — but only from `qL >= 1024`. Probe, 24/4 heads,
  kL 22,747, bf16, per call:

  | mlx | qL | default dispatch | `force_fused` |
  |---|---:|---|---|
  | 0.32.2 | 512 | +533 MiB, 20.1 ms | +6 MiB, 17.1 ms |
  | 0.32.2 | 2048 | +2127 MiB, 80.7 ms | +24 MiB, 67.7 ms |
  | 0.32.3 | 512 | +444 MiB, 20.2 ms | fused, 17.5 ms |
  | 0.32.3 | 2048 | fused, 67.8 ms | fused, 67.8 ms |

  So **`0013` stays**: `lean` prefills at 512, and the last chunk of almost
  every prompt is shorter than 1024. The start banner still reports `0013`
  active on 0.32.3.
- `#4380` / `#4431` (sdpa_vector for GQA 12/16) — Qwen3.8 is GQA 6.
- Measured throughput is unchanged (table above); the greedy baseline would
  move for nothing.

Revisit when an mlx release carries something for NAX / GQA 6, or when the
`mlx-vlm` floor rises above 0.32.2.
